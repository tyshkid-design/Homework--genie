The process

From "I'm stuck" to submitted, in four steps.

No lengthy onboarding, no browsing profiles. Upload the task, and we take it from there.

1

Upload your task

Create a student account, then upload the assignment file along with a short note on what kind of support you need and when it's due.

2

Get matched

Your task is reviewed and matched with a Tasker who fits the subject and the type of help you asked for — no need to search or interview anyone yourself.

3

Work through it together

Your Tasker works through the assignment with you, explaining the reasoning rather than just handing back an answer, so it actually sticks.

4

Review and move on

Everything you've submitted and received stays in your dashboard, so you can review it, track its status, and come back to it later if you need to.

Questions

Before you get started

How fast will I get matched with a Tasker?
Most tasks are matched within minutes during regular hours. If you upload late at night, it may take a little longer, but support runs 24/7.
What file types can I upload?
PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, plain text and zip files, up to around 20MB, directly from the assignment upload form.
Is my account only for one type of support?
No — one student account covers every service, from proofreading to exam prep. You just describe what you need for each task you submit.
Can I track the status of what I've submitted?
Yes — your dashboard shows every assignment as pending, in progress or completed, and you can download your files any time.
