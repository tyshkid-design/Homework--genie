Our story

Homework Genie started with one very late night.

An assignment due at 8am, a question nobody could answer at midnight, and a realization that "office hours" don't run on a student's actual schedule.

✦

Why we built this

Learning doesn't stop being confusing just because the library closed. We built Homework Genie so getting unstuck doesn't depend on the time of day, or on knowing the right person to ask.

Every task that comes through gets matched to someone who actually knows the subject — not a generic answer key, not a chatbot script.

“

We're not trying to do the work for anyone. We're trying to make sure nobody's stuck at 11pm with no one to ask.

HG
