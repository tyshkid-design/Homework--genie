A closer look

Two ways students use Homework Genie most

A

Assignment Support

Most requests start the same way: a task that made sense in class and stopped making sense at 9pm. You upload it, explain where you're stuck, and a Tasker who knows the subject works through it with you.

  • Works with the assignment you already have, not a generic template
  • Explains the reasoning, not just the answer
  • Turnaround built around your actual deadline
B

Exam Preparation

The second most common ask: a test coming up and not enough of a plan. Taskers help build a study plan, work through practice questions, and flag the topics worth another pass.

  • Practice questions in the same format as your exam
  • Focus on the topics that are actually weak, not everything
  • Available for a last-minute session, not just weeks ahead
